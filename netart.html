GodzillaVs.

My idea I tried tackling with this project was like an interactive comic, where for example two characters such as Godzilla and Mechagodzilla fighting while adding bits of narration and overall my own commentary throughout the fight, This is very well a good example of how I handle the hyperlinks having them labeled to make them have some sort of suspense to them as your progressing through the pages to find out what happens next within the even that's taking place. I would want the viewer to experience having fun with this and just overall treat this as if it were a webcomic of some sorts. There is this one technical challenge that I did encounter, when I was deploying the official site the image on the 6th page didn't show, which I later realized that it was how I labeled the description of one of the images, which also shared the same description as another image, even though it would still show locally, but when I realized it it fixed overall and showed on the fully deployed site..
