Remix

For this project I combined a mock up character select screen from the hit arcade game Marvel Vs. Capcom with two random characters that I could think of that come from series of media that are polar opposites from each other, that being Ed from Ed,Edd and Eddy to Spawn and Spawn himself. This is paying homage to the known 2D fighting game engine that has a significant spot in its online presence, highlighting its absurdity containing many different characters from all sorts of media in its randomness thats mainly from the free public domain for users to add/create basically any character, stage, lifebars and screen scenery in many different styles. There is several YouTube videos of this fighting engine that has been around since 1999 showing many characters from many different origins just casually fighting it out (ex: Homer Vs. Goku)..
